Kitty Knight – Tiny Knight Survivors

One tiny knight. An unreasonable number of monsters. Kitty Knight is a free, cute pixel-art survivor roguelite you can play right in your browser – no download needed, on phone or desktop.

Guide a tiny kitten in oversized armor (yes, the viral cat-in-armor meme brought to life) as it swings a giant sword, collects XP, levels up, unlocks 24 weapons and 22 tomes, evolves its gear, and takes on five bosses – all the way to the Cat Eater.
